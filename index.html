THE PERSON BEHIND THE LENS

A life spent
looking up.

Rohan is an amateur aviation photographer and aviation enthusiast originally from Kolhapur, India. He now lives in Esslingen, Germany. For the past six years, he has photographed various aircrafts and captured the moments that keep him looking skyward.

An automotive engineer by profession, Rohan is fascinated by the design and character of machines, whether on the road or in the air. His favorite aircraft is the Sukhoi Su-30MKI. Through Chasing Mach, he shares a selection of his aviation photographs and the aircraft, places, and moments behind them.
